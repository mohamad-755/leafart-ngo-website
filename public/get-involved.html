Ways to Help

Volunteer With I Leaf Art

Every mural is a team effort. Here are the main ways people join in.

Join a Mural Day

No experience needed — show up, get a brush, and help bring a design to life alongside our lead artists and the local community.

Help Run a Workshop

Support our school and youth workshops — helping kids design, prep walls, and paint safely from start to finish.

Lend a Skill

Photography, social media, translation, logistics, fundraising — mural days need more than painters. Tell us what you're good at.

Sign Up to Volunteer

Tell us a bit about yourself and we'll reach out about the next mural day or workshop near you.

A Lebanese non-profit based in Beirut Lebanon, I Leaf Art transforms neglected walls into vibrant community murals, bringing together artists, volunteers, different settings, communities and families across Lebanon to create meaningful, colorful, safe spaces through art.
