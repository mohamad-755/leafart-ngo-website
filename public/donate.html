What Donations Achieve

Murals in Communities

Large-scale public artwork that reclaims neglected spaces and lifts neighborhoods.

Community-Led

Every Dollar Counts

Your gift goes directly to art supplies, artist pay, and mural costs.

A Lebanese non-profit based in Beirut Lebanon, I Leaf Art transforms neglected walls into vibrant community murals, bringing together artists, volunteers, different settings, communities and families across Lebanon to create meaningful, colorful, safe spaces through art.
