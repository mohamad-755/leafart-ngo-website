Why LeafArt?

More Than Paint on a Wall

We believe public art is a public good. A mural can reclaim a neglected corner, spark conversation, and give a neighborhood a renewed sense of identity and belonging.

LeafArt partners directly with local artists, schools, and residents so that every project reflects the people who live there. We pay artists fairly, source sustainable materials, and design each mural through community workshops.

The result is art that doesn't just decorate a space — it transforms how people feel about where they live.
